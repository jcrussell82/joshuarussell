Overview

Performance with proof

Cascade CBX makes certified cannabinoid products for athletes who care about performance and about what they put in their bodies.

I built a stronger digital and campaign direction around that idea, combining bold product storytelling, athlete-focused creative, certification messaging and a more confident visual system across web, social and motion.

What I owned
  • Campaign creative direction and messaging
  • Social and paid creative across formats
  • AI-assisted animated product sequences, made with Claude and Higgsfield
  • Editing promotional video in Adobe Premiere Rush
  • Homepage design that carries the campaign onto the site
The campaign

Earned. Trusted. Tested.

Certification as the hook

NSF Certified for Sport, and the banned substances it screens for, became the headline, not a footnote.

Athletes first

Athlete-focused creative with short, earned-it lines carries the attitude.

A bold, flexible system

Heavy condensed type, a teal accent and a cream alternate let one look stretch from product posts to promotions.

Motion that sells the product

AI-assisted product animation and short edits give the roll-on product energy in feeds and on the site.
