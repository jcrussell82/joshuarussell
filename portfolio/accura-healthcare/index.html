Overview

Helping families find care

Accura HealthCare’s site was dated and hard to navigate, and there was no easy way for families to find a nearby nursing home or assisted-living community.

Working within Accura’s existing logo and brand guidelines, I redesigned the layout, flow and visual design: a clearer hierarchy, defined calls to action, and search and sort tools for finding facilities by location.

Key decisions

Clearer paths to the right care

Clear hierarchy

Reorganized content and navigation make essential information easy to reach.

Find care nearby
