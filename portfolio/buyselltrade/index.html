Overview

One place to buy, sell and trade

My client used several platforms to buy, sell and trade collectibles like Funko Pops and trading cards. He wanted one simpler, better experience for collectors like himself.

I designed a marketplace where fans can buy, sell and trade their collectibles, with a sense of community that lets them share and appreciate each other’s collections.

What I owned
  • Marketplace and seller dashboard design
  • A user flow for the most common tasks
  • A 24-screen interactive prototype
  • The BuySellTrade logo and design system
Key decisions

Built for collectors

Shopping that’s fun

Big product imagery, category shortcuts and a playful order confirmation keep buying enjoyable.

Price history

Price history and comparisons help collectors judge what a piece is worth.

Seller tools

A dashboard for sales, listings, offers and billing, with fewer steps to complete each task.

Community

Built-in chat keeps buyers, sellers and traders talking.

Brand and design system

The BuySellTrade identity

Logo

A clean, modern mark built around an integrated “B.” Just as the B merges with itself, the community connects with one another, forming a network of enthusiasts who share a passion for collecting.

Color, type and interface

A bold purple paired with white keeps the brand vibrant and modern. Clean sans-serifs, with Industry for headings, give it a contemporary look, and dynamic icons add interest. On the website, a dark interface with bright purple accents makes the collectibles stand out while keeping navigation clear.
