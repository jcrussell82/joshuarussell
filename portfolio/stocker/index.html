Overview

Making insurance feel approachable

Insurance websites often overwhelm visitors with dense content, generic messaging and unclear navigation. Stocker needed something simpler that still carried the professionalism expected from an established independent agency.

I handled everything from the logo, fonts and colors to the site itself, worked through revisions with the client and account manager, and prototyped the site with Claude before launch.

Key decisions

Simpler, more personal, more credible

Cleaner hierarchy

Clearly defined business and personal service categories replace dense lists of coverage.

A stronger value proposition

“Reliable coverage, real peace of mind” leads, with service and simplicity over lowest price.

Authentic imagery

Real people and a personal introduction to Jim Stocker make the agency feel human.

Every device

Intuitive navigation and modern type keep the site easy to explore on any screen.

Brand identity

Logo, color and type

I designed the Stocker identity end to end, from the logo to the colors and typography that carry through the site, so the agency feels established, personal and consistent.

Color and typography

A deep navy paired with a warm gold, with modern typography, gives the agency a professional, trustworthy look that still feels personal.
