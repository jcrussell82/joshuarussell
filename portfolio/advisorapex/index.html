Overview

Making a specialist process self-serve

Life insurance professionals use mortality illustrations and probability modeling to judge risk and recommend coverage. The formulas behind them were run by a specialist, which made the process hard to scale and slowed agents down.

The client wanted to turn that expertise into a product agents could use on their own. My job was to organize a highly technical calculation process into a clear, approachable experience, and to give the new platform a credible brand.

What I owned
  • Translating the client’s subject-matter expertise into a digital workflow
  • User flow, wireframes and high-fidelity interface design
  • An interactive Adobe XD prototype
  • The AdvisorApex logo and visual identity
Key decisions

Making the calculations feel manageable

A guided flow

The experience runs from data entry through calculation to results, so users always know what’s needed and where they are.

Focused inputs

Inputs are grouped into small sections instead of one long form, which reduces the load of an unfamiliar technical process.

Readable results

Life-duration and cash-flow outputs are presented as structured charts and tables that agents can review with clients.

A credible identity

A forward-pointing mark, royal blue and dark gray, and Poppins give the platform a clean, dependable feel.

Brand and design system

The AdvisorApex identity

Logo

I created the AdvisorApex logo to communicate growth, progress and professional expertise. The mark incorporates an abstract forward-pointing arrow, giving the identity a clean, contemporary feel. Blue and gray convey the trust and stability that matter in insurance and financial services.

Color and typography

Royal blue and dark gray form the foundation, with lighter blues, silver tones and supporting accents adding contrast to distinguish actions, information and interface states. Poppins was chosen for its clean geometric structure, and its range of weights supports a clear hierarchy across headings, labels and form fields.

Visual language

Structured layouts, generous spacing, consistent interface components and supporting illustrations make the technical subject matter feel more accessible while reinforcing AdvisorApex as a modern, dependable tool.
