Overview

Everything about your home, under one roof

Homeowners struggle to keep track of the documents, notes, photos and project details that come with a house, and there’s no easy way to hand that information to a buyer when they sell.

I designed a mobile app where homeowners can store and organize everything about their home, from paint colors and appliances to receipts and a timeline of work, and connect with contractors for new projects. Orman Guidance led the user research, including a competitive analysis, a usability study and a stakeholder brainstorm, which shaped the brand and the app.

What I owned
  • Mobile UX and UI for both homeowners and contractors
  • A 73-screen interactive prototype
  • The WOMS logo, colors, type and iconography
Key decisions

Designed around the home, not the paperwork

Organized by room

Pictures, notes, tags, items, videos and documents live in each room, so details like a paint color are quick to find.

Quick add

One central button adds a photo, note, document, item or project from anywhere in the app.

Two sides, one app

Contractors get their own profile, dashboard and leads, while homeowners can request bids and check availability.

Under one roof

A sideways “W” that reads as both a roof and an arrow, with greens for growth and Roboto and Poppins type.

Brand and design system

The WOMS identity

Logo

A distinctive sideways “W” abstracts both a roof and an arrow, reflecting the idea of keeping everything about the home under one roof.

Color and typography

Shades of green bring a sense of growth and energy. Roboto headers give a modern touch, and Poppins body text keeps things readable while staying contemporary.
