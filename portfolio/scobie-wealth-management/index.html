Overview

Designing for trust and conversion

Scobie Wealth Management has built its reputation over more than three decades. The website needed to reflect that history: establishing trust quickly, communicating the firm’s values and giving prospective clients clear ways to connect.

The design pairs a refined visual system with a more focused user journey, using credibility, social proof and well-placed calls to action throughout.

The approach

Building confidence at every step

Trust and credibility

The firm’s experience, client relationships and long-term approach come forward throughout the site.

Clearer engagement

Intuitive navigation and prominent calls to action make it easy for prospects to take the next step.

Brand storytelling

Mountain imagery, natural colors and messaging around stewardship and longevity tie the site back to the firm.

Conversion focus

Key pages are built on clear hierarchy, social proof and accessible consultation opportunities.

Extending the brand

From a logo to a visual language

Working from Scobie’s existing logo, I created the visual language around it: the colors, typography, icons and motifs that carry through the site.

The mountain motif

The mountain from the logo became a recurring visual motif, set into the backgrounds of key sections and the footer to connect every page back to the firm’s identity.

Color and icons

I brought in a lighter tan and a lighter green alongside the brand’s deep green, plus a set of icons, giving the site a warmer, more approachable palette.
