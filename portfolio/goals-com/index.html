Overview

One platform for goals, incentives and sales performance

Goals.com is a sales-performance platform that helps organizations align teams, track progress and build accountability through goals, incentives, reporting and CRM tools.

I was its lead designer over many years as it grew from a personal goal tracker into a full business and sales-management product. Working closely with the product owner, developers and leadership, I turned business requirements and feedback into dashboards, workflows and interactive prototypes, and kept the experience as consistent as possible while the product changed quickly.

What I owned
  • Translating requirements from the product owner and leadership into flows and screens
  • Dashboards, reporting, goals, incentives and CRM workflows
  • Desktop and mobile interfaces and interactive Figma prototypes
  • The setup and conversation experience for Ace, the AI sales coach
  • Supporting brand, email and app-store marketing assets
Key decisions

Keeping a fast-moving product usable

Dashboards first

Sales totals, pipeline, goal progress and team leaderboards are organized into scannable dashboards for managers and reps.

Motivating incentives

Bonuses, contests and commissions are clear flows, from setting them up to tracking who’s winning.

CRM in the same place

Leads, opportunities, customers, accounts and messaging sit inside the same product on desktop and mobile.

An AI sales coach

I designed the setup and conversational experience for Ace, the AI coach built into the platform.

Shared patterns

A supporting color system for status and alerts, Roboto type and reusable cards kept new features familiar as the product grew.

Brand and design system

Expanding the Goals.com identity

Rather than redesigning the existing logo, I expanded and maintained a cohesive visual identity across the platform. As Goals.com evolved, I developed consistent interface patterns, color systems, typography and reusable components that reinforced the brand while supporting usability.

Color palette

To complement the established primary blue, I developed a supporting color system of yellow, orange, green, ocean and red to communicate status, progress, alerts and key actions consistently throughout the platform.

Typography

Roboto was chosen as the primary typeface for its readability across dashboards, forms and reporting. Consistent type established a clear visual hierarchy and reinforced the platform’s clean, modern look.

Visual elements

Iconography, spacing, card layouts and reusable interface components created a consistent visual language, improving recognition and keeping the experience cohesive as new features were added over time.
