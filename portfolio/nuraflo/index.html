Overview

AI that helps you understand, not just produce

Most AI tools help people create more. Nuraflo explores how AI could help people understand themselves better through the words they already write, while balancing privacy, transparency and emotional trust.

I designed the brand and an end-to-end product experience, from the marketing site through onboarding, dashboards, behavioral insights, personalization and reflection tools.

What I owned
  • Product strategy and information architecture
  • Brand identity and design system
  • UX/UI design across web and app
  • Interactive prototyping and AI experience design
Key decisions

Designing for reflection, not productivity

Trust up front

Onboarding explains what’s connected and lets people tune tone and preferences before any analysis happens.

Calm, dark dashboards

Abstract ideas made visible

The Relationship Radar and Tone Tracker turn emotional patterns into visuals people can actually read.

Brand and design system

A thoughtful companion, not another tool

Nuraflo was envisioned as a companion for personal growth rather than another productivity tool. Every design decision reinforced clarity, reflection and emotional trust.

Logo

The mark forms an abstract “N” while also creating a fluid, continuous shape that represents the flow of communication, insight and personal transformation. Rather than leaning on typical AI symbolism, it stays simple and human, paired with a blue-to-teal palette and clean type.

Color and typography

Soft blues establish clarity and confidence while teal accents suggest growth and reflection. Dark interfaces reduce fatigue during long reading sessions. A modern geometric sans with generous spacing and clear hierarchy keeps lengthy AI explanations and reflection prompts readable on desktop and mobile.

Visual elements

Recurring circular motifs represent continuous growth, while clean cards, subtle gradients and generous whitespace reduce cognitive load. Visualizations like the Relationship Radar and Tone Tracker turn abstract emotional ideas into something approachable.
