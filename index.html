Designer, art director, problem solver.

For twelve years, I was the lead and sole designer at a digital agency, owning the design direction across products, websites, brands and campaigns while collaborating closely with leadership, account managers and developers.

What motivates me

The projects that stay with me are the ones that make a tangible difference, like designing for Smile Network, which helps children receive cleft-lip surgery in underserved communities, and Always Lucy Strong, which supports families affected by ALS. Good design isn’t just about better interfaces. It helps great organizations extend their impact.

Before that I spent seven years at ADG Promo. Today I also lead Thinkigy, my independent studio, supporting select organizations with product design, websites and branding, and launching my own products with AI-assisted development.
